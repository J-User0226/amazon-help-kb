# Amazon Merchant Fulfilled Services Program Policy

The Merchant Fulfilled Services Policy (this “Policy”) governs your participation in the Merchant Fulfilled Services program (“MFS”). This Policy supplements the terms of the [Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1791?locale=en-US) (the “Agreement”), which continues to govern your access to and use of the Services. If there is a conflict between this Policy and the Agreement, the conflict will be resolved by giving precedence in the following order: this Policy and the Agreement. All capitalized terms not defined in this Policy have the respective meanings set forth in the Agreement.

**Eligibility**

MFS is an invite-only program that allows you (if invited) to offer services (for example installation, assembly, haul-away, or similar services) to customers in Amazon’s store in connection with the products you sell in Amazon’s store, and to perform those services at customer properties (the “MFS Services”).

**Workmanship**

You will, at a minimum, successfully complete all MFS Services in a timely, workmanlike, and professional manner in accordance with (a) the level of professional care customarily observed by highly skilled professionals rendering similar services, (b) any applicable manufacturer warranties, and (c) all applicable Laws including business and professional licensing requirements, building and zoning codes, safety and health Laws, industry standard safety requirements, and state-specific “Call Before You Dig” or similar requirements concerning underground utility lines. You will provide all equipment and supplies required to perform MFS Services.

The workmanship of each MFS Service you provide to a customer (each a “Job”) will be warranted by us to the customer pursuant to the [Happiness Guarantee](https://www.amazon.in/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202132790). In the event that a customer files a Happiness Guarantee claim or any other claim relating to any of your Jobs, you must cooperate with the customer and us (including adhering to the Happiness Guarantee policies and help content on Seller Central) to remedy the issue at your expense, which may include, at our sole discretion, a re-performance of the Job. Accordingly, the workmanship of each Job will be warranted by you to us for the longer of:

- one year from the date of successful Job completion; or
- such period as Amazon may be required under Law to warrant the workmanship of the Job to the customer.

**Tech App**

We will provide an app to support the successful completion of Jobs to customers (the “Tech App”). You and Your Personnel are expected to use the Tech App on your own mobile device(s) at your own expense. The Tech App will enable you and Your Personnel to, for example:

- view, manage, and mark Jobs as complete;
- communicate with customers to verify Job details and send an ETA notification;
- navigate to the customer’s property; and
- take photos and selfie verification

We may establish requirements regarding the use of the Tech App, and you will use the Tech App in accordance with our instructions when providing the MFS Services.

As MFS evolves to meet the needs of customers, we may make periodic updates to the Tech App, at our sole discretion, and we will notify you of the changes. You must adopt these changes promptly at your sole cost and expense. Any use of your own equivalent to the Tech App, in place of our Tech App, must be approved in writing at our sole discretion. If approved, such equivalent tool must be connected to our systems using an application programming interface. Such equivalent tool will be referred to as “API” in this Policy. Certain capabilities may only be available in the Tech App.

**General Standards**

While performing the MFS Services, you must ensure a safe and professional experience for customers and uphold Amazon’s reputation, including by meeting the SLS described above and the additional expectations described below and in the Agreement.

**Safeguarding customers, their expectations, and Amazon’s reputation while performing MFS Services**

In order to safeguard customers, their expectations, and Amazon’s reputation while performing the MFS Services, and to successfully complete Jobs, you must ensure that you and Your Personnel:

- Are fully trained on the SLS, understand how to maintain minimum SLS, and reliably complete Jobs.
- Behave respectfully and professionally when completing Jobs, including when interacting with customers and the general public.
- Fully understand the scope of work for each MFS Service they are authorized to perform.
- Send their ETA through the Tech App or API 30 to 60 minutes prior to the start of the arrival window.
- Honor the customer’s preferred arrival window. We require that you arrive at the customer’s property to perform the MFS Services during the customer’s preferred arrival window as found in Manage Orders > Order Details in Seller Central.
- Never perform in-home services while an unaccompanied minor is inside the home. You will ensure that a child’s parent or guardian is present at all times.
- Select “Start Job” in the Tech App or API to signal when an appointment has started.
- Review the scope of work in-person with the customer prior to starting work on the Job and check the environment for factors that may lead to an unsafe installation or improper setup. Do not provide any MFS Services that are inconsistent with safety standards or Law.
- Take appropriate and reasonable precautions to protect the customer and their property from harm and/or damage.
- Discuss and clearly communicate Change Orders (defined below) with the customer before starting a Job or as soon as the need for a change is discovered.
- Reliably perform the full scope of work for every Job.
- Confirm the customer is satisfied, in person, with the completed Job, and correct or improve the Job if not. After the customer confirms they are satisfied, mark the Job complete in the Tech App or API prior to leaving the customer’s property. All completed Jobs must be marked complete (when applicable) in the Tech App or API, or as otherwise indicated by us.
- Take and submit a selfie using the Tech App or API when prompted.
- Only use their Amazon account (“log-in”) and not the log-in of any other individual or divulge their log-in to another individual.
- Not in any way represent to customers that you or Your Personnel are Amazon or that you or Your Personnel are acting on behalf of Amazon.
- Abide by Amazon’s Global Personnel Suitability Requirements (the “Suitability Requirements,” noting that “Supplier” in the Suitability Requirements means you, “Purchaser” means us, “Contingent Worker” means you or Your Personnel, and references in the Suitability Requirements to providing Services to Purchaser (or performing Services for Purchaser) means, for purposes of this Policy, your enrollment in MFS and performance of MFS Services) posted at [https://aws.amazon.com/legal/global-personnel-suitability-requirements/](https://aws.amazon.com/legal/global-personnel-suitability-requirements/) (as updated from time to time).

Additionally, Your Personnel that will perform Jobs on-site at a customer’s location must:

- Be at least 18 years old and have a valid drivers’ license of the type required to operate the applicable motor vehicle;
- Sign and agree to consent forms that allow your company to share with us the results of the drug and background tests, performance management/disciplinary actions, and any other information that we may request in connection with exercising our audit rights;
- Sign and agree to a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement to protect customer and Amazon Confidential Information;
- Bring all appropriate tools, materials, and equipment needed to complete each Job during the scheduled appointment;
- Refrain from seeking gratuity;
- Be authorized to lawfully perform the MFS Services pursuant to applicable immigration and work status Laws; and
- Abide by all applicable Laws while performing the Job.

Your Personnel can perform MFS Services only if they are eligible and remain eligible to perform MFS Services pursuant to the background check, drug testing, and other procedures and requirements set forth in this Policy. You will not allow Your Personnel who are ineligible, or who become ineligible, to perform MFS Services. You also agree that any access granted to our Licensed Materials (defined below) or customers will be automatically revoked with respect to any of Your Personnel who are ineligible without the need for affirmative action by or notice from us. In addition, you will discontinue an individual’s assignment to perform MFS Services under this Policy immediately after receiving notice from us that we believe in good faith that (a) such of Your Personnel is or has become ineligible to perform MFS Services, or (b) a commercially reasonable basis exists for not assigning or discontinuing the assignment of such of Your Personnel (for example, a customer complaint; to initiate or complete an investigation of alleged wrongdoing by such of Your Personnel; etc.). This is expressly limited to the eligibility of Your Personnel to perform MFS Services and does not impose any obligation on you to make any employment decision, such as any hiring or termination decision. Further, this does not prevent you from assigning Your Personnel to provide services outside of MFS.

With respect to Your Personnel:

- Your Personnel that you assign to perform MFS Services must be employees as opposed to independent contractors. You must not engage any independent contractors or any other subcontractors to perform the MFS Services without our prior written consent.
- You are required to maintain appropriate records and acknowledge in the onboarding process that a 5-panel drug test (where allowed by Law) has been administered and passed by each of Your Personnel.
- You will also pay all amounts required under any workers’ compensation acts, disability benefit acts, unemployment insurance acts, and any other employee benefit acts when due.

- You will retain:
  - Full control over the manner in which you and Your Personnel perform all MFS Services;
  - Exclusive control over Your Personnel;
  - Exclusive control over your labor and employee relations and policies relating to wages, hours, working conditions and other employment conditions; and
  - Exclusive right to hire, transfer, suspend, lay off, recall, promote, discipline, discharge, and adjust grievances with Your Personnel.

- You are solely responsible for all salaries and other compensation of Your Personnel, and for making all deductions and withholdings from your employees’ salaries and other compensation and paying all contributions, taxes, and assessments. You and Your Personnel are not entitled to and are not eligible to participate in any workers’ compensation, retirement, insurance, stock options, or any other benefits afforded to Amazon employees.
- You will be solely responsible for all theft, damage, and/or misconduct related to the acts or omissions of Your Personnel.

**Change Order**

If you identify additional work is necessary to complete a Job to the customer’s satisfaction beyond what is in the approved scope of work, you may add a reasonable and appropriate change fee in addition to the original MFS Service price, but only with the customer’s prior approval (“Change Order”). Customer approval and payment must be processed through the Tech App or API.

**Customer Communication**

You must use an Amazon communication channel (for example, Tech App or Buyer-Seller Messaging) for all communication with a customer regarding their MFS Services. You may not use customer personal data or an Amazon communication channel to contact customers in any way for marketing or promotional purposes, or for reasons unrelated to providing the requested MFS Services.

**Non-solicitation**

While on customer property, you must not perform solicitation of any kind, including the distribution of any literature or materials; wearing clothes for the specific purpose of solicitation (excluding your standard uniforms); the sale of merchandise, products, or services; seeking financial contributions, memberships, subscriptions, or signatures; or the distribution of advertisements or other commercial materials.

**Setoff and Rights to Withhold**

We may delay or withhold payment of any sums due and payable to you on account of any failure by you to perform in accordance with this Policy and the Agreement, including Happiness Guarantee claims or other customer claims attributed to your noncompliance with this Policy or the Agreement, or for other reasonable cause (including to offset any damages, costs, or expenses incurred by us on account of any default by you with respect to any representations, warranties, or obligations set forth in this Policy and the Agreement). Further, we may recover any amounts owed by you to customers or us by any method provided in the Agreement, and you agree we may offset any and all amounts we may owe you against any and all amounts you owe us.

**Audit**

While you are enrolled in MFS and for three years thereafter, you will keep true and accurate books and records relating to your and Your Personnel’s performance of MFS Services (collectively, “Records”). All Records will be kept in accordance with generally accepted auditing standards. At our request, you will (a) provide us with access to, and permit us to examine, the Records, which may be accomplished, at our option, through electronic means, the delivery of copies of the requested Records to our designated address, or onsite at your facilities; and (b) permit us or our designated representatives to inspect and audit (during normal business hours) your and your subcontractors’ facilities, processes, systems, and workplace conditions for compliance with this Policy. We may make and retain copies of all Records examined regardless of which method of access to Records we select and may document (including through photography and videography) the results of any inspection or audit.
