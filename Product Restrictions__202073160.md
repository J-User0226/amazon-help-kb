# Product Restrictions

All Sellers participating in the Subscribe with Amazon Program must adhere to the following policies.

## Restricted Products
The sale of illegal, unsafe, and other Restricted Products listed on these pages, is strictly prohibited. If you violate these restrictions, payments to you may be withheld or forfeited. The sale of illegal or unsafe products can also lead to legal action, including civil and criminal penalties.

"**Restricted Products**" means the items described as "Restricted Products" in these Program Guidelines and other items so designated by Amazon, in its discretion. The following are considered "Restricted Products" and may not be sold via the Subscribe with Amazon Program:
- Offensive Products: Items that Amazon deems offensive. Amazon reserves the right to determine the appropriateness of Product Listings on its site, and remove any Product and Product Listing at any time. Examples of prohibited listings within this category include products that (i) promote or glorify hatred, violence, racial, sexual or religious intolerance or promote organizations with such views, (ii) encompass videos, sound or other recordings taken without the subject's permission, and (ii) promote or undertake illegal activities.
- Sexually Explicit Products: Products that portray nudity in a gratuitous or graphic manner, such as: pornography, X-rated content and "hard-core material."
