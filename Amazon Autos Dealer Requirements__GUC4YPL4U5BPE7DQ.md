# Amazon Autos Dealer Requirements

1. **Compensation**. Section S-4 “Compensation” of the Agreement does not apply to you or the Program.

2. **Excluded Products Waiver**. Notwithstanding any Excluded Products in the Agreement, you may list and sell or lease New, Used or OEM CPO motor vehicles on the Amazon Site as long as you are participating in the Program according to the [Autos Program Policies](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/GXE3YGZ54YHJ6RB9).

3. **Your Product Listing Requirements**.

a. Pricing.

i. For the purposes of these Dealer Requirements under the Autos Program Policies the definition of “Seller Price” is as follows:

"**Seller Price**" means the listed sale price of a vehicle which includes all fees other than taxes, government fees, and any other fees that seller is prohibited from including in the offering price by applicable law.

ii. In addition to the requirements set forth in (c) below, you will provide: (1) an itemized Seller Price which will include the itemized price of any dealer installed equipment, seller fees (i.e. processing, administrative, conveyance, servicing, closing or similar fees) and any other non-government fees included in the Seller Price, as permitted or required by applicable law; and (2) an itemized list of any additional seller fees not included in the Seller Price payable by customer, as required by applicable law. Any fees that are permitted to be included in the Seller Price shall be included in the Seller Price.

iii. The Seller Price will not include any Original Equipment Manufacturer rebates or any third-party rebates.

iv. If you elect to make available any dealer incentive (e.g. dealer cash, dealer hold back, or other such incentive), you must include such incentive in the Seller Price.

v. You will not discriminate against Amazon in any way, including but not limited to, by providing incentives that may only be used for in-person sales and not for online sales.

vi. Upon a customer submitting their credit application, unless such customer otherwise completes and provides all required documentation and completes payment (if applicable), you will honor the Seller Price listed on the Amazon Site at the time of customer’s submission of the credit application for at least 27 business hours.

vii. Notwithstanding the “Policy on Reference Prices”, Amazon will not compute a “Was Price” for any ASINs listed as part of the Program.

b. **Product**. You may list New, Used and OEM CPO vehicles, excluding any vehicles containing systems that would violate 15 CFR Part 791. “**New**” means vehicles that are defined as new under applicable law, manufactured by an OEM, and which you are authorized by such OEM to sell. “**Used**” means vehicles that are not New or OEM CPO or defined as used under applicable law. “**OEM CPO**” means used vehicles that are inspected and reconditioned in accordance with an OEM’s established certified pre-owned program.

c. **Product Information. **Section S-1.1 of the Agreement is hereby deleted and replaced with the following:

“**S-1.1 Products and Product Information.** You will provide accurate and complete Required Product Information for each vehicle or service that you offer through any Amazon Site and promptly update that information as necessary to ensure it at all times remains accurate and complete. Any Required Product Information will be delivered in the form required by Amazon. If you do not provide the Required Vehicle Information in the form required by Amazon, you agree that Amazon may provide (via third-party or otherwise) the Required Vehicle Information in the required form; provided that, if Amazon provides the Required Vehicle Information you will inform Amazon of any errors or issues in the Required Vehicle Information within 24 hours of such a listing being published on the Amazon Site by contacting your account representative. Amazon will correct any error within 12 hours of such notice. If you do not inform Amazon of any error or issue in the Required Vehicle Information within 24 hours, such listing will be deemed accurate and complete and you will be responsible for the Required Product Information. You will also ensure that Your Materials, Your Products (including packaging) and your offer and subsequent sale of any of the same on any Amazon Site comply with all applicable Laws (including all minimum age, marking and labeling requirements) and do not contain any sexually explicit (except to the extent expressly permitted under our applicable Program Policies), defamatory or obscene materials. Except as otherwise provided in the Autos Program Policies or Dealer Requirements, you may not provide any information for, or otherwise seek to offer any Excluded Products on any Amazon Sites; or provide any URL Marks for use, or request that any URL Marks be used, on any Amazon Site. If you offer a product for sale on an Amazon Site that requires a warning under California Health & Safety Code Section 25249.6 (a “Proposition 65 Warning”) you (a) will provide us with such warning in the manner specified in our Program Policies, (b) agree that our display of a Proposition 65 Warning on a product detail page is confirmation of our receipt of that warning, and (c) will only revise or remove a Proposition 65 Warning for a product when the prior warning is no longer legally required.”

d. **Required Product Information**. For the purposes of these Dealer Requirements, the Autos Program Policies, and the Agreement the definition of “Required Product Information” is hereby deleted from the Agreement and replaced with the following:

**"Required Product Information"** means, with respect to each of Your Products in connection with the Amazon Site, the following: (a) description, including as applicable, location-specific availability and options, scheduling guidelines and service cancellation policies; (b) SKU, stock number and UPC/EAN/JAN numbers, and other identifying information as Amazon may reasonably request; (c) information regarding in-stock status and availability, shipping limitations or requirements, and Shipment Information (in each case, in accordance with any categorizations prescribed by Amazon from time to time); (d) categorization within each Amazon product category and browse structure as prescribed by Amazon from time to time; (e) digitized images that accurately depicts only Your Product in accordance with the image policy, available at [https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/vehicles/seller_facing_content/Amazon_Autos_-_Image_guidelines_2024.pdf](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/vehicles/seller_facing_content/Amazon_Autos_-_Image_guidelines_2024.pdf), including a main image and at least 14 additional digitized images of the interior and exterior of a vehicle, each of which complies with all Amazon image guidelines, and does not include any additional logos, text or other markings; (f) Purchase Price; (g) shipping and handling charge (in accordance with our standard functionality); (h) any text, disclaimers, warnings, notices, labels, warranties, or other content required by applicable Law to be displayed, or that are necessary for the safe use of Your Product, in connection with the offer, merchandising, advertising, or sale of Your Product; (i) any vendor requirements, restocking fees or other terms and conditions applicable to such product that a customer should be aware of prior to purchasing the product; (j) brand or vehicle make; (k) model; (l) a delimited list of technical specifications; (m) SKU and UPC/EAN/JAN numbers (and other identifying information as we may reasonably request) for accessories related to Your Product that is available in our catalog; (n) the state or country Your Product ships from; (o) vehicle identification number (“**VIN”**); (p) model year; (q) vehicle exterior color; (r) dealer identification number (unique seller identifier set by inventory provider); (s) vehicle trim; (t) OEM installed options; (u) dealer installed options; (v) modification date; (w) vehicle exterior color standardized to Amazon’s set guidelines for color; (x) vehicle mileage; (y) vehicle condition (e.g. New, Used or OEM CPO); (z) vehicle condition note (if applicable); (aa) base vehicle price (base manufacturer’s suggested retail price (**“MSRP”)** without any OEM installed packages); (bb) total MSRP of the vehicle (including OEM installed items); (cc) Seller Price and any other price inputs required by the pricing tool; (dd) any discount, incentive rebate, promotional offer, or other term of offer and/or sale that you or the OEM make available through the Amazon Site; (ee) Required Vehicle Information; (ff) Buyer’s Guide for Used or OEM CPO; and (gg) any other information reasonably requested by us (e.g., the condition of used or refurbished products; and invoices and other documentation demonstrating the safety and authenticity of Your Products).”

e. **Required Vehicle Information**. For the purposes of these Dealer Requirements, the Autos Program Policies, and the Agreement the following definition of “Required Vehicle Information” is hereby added to the Agreement:

**"Required Vehicle Information” **means, each attribute listed in [Required Vehicle Information](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/GHTLL7GRVSKV5PTU).”

f. **Images**. You will provide digital images that depict Your Product in accordance with the image policy, available at: [https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/vehicles/seller_facing_content/Amazon_Autos_-_Image_guidelines_2024.pdf](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/vehicles/seller_facing_content/Amazon_Autos_-_Image_guidelines_2024.pdf)

If you do not provide digital images in the form required by Amazon, you agree that Amazon may provide (via third-party or otherwise) digital images; provided that, if Amazon provides any digital images you will inform Amazon of any errors or issues with the digital images within 24 hours of such a listing being published on the Amazon Site by contacting your account representative. Amazon will correct any error or remove any digital images within 12 hours of such notice. If you do not inform Amazon of any error or issue with the digital images within 24 hours, such listing will be deemed accurate and complete and you will be responsible for the Required Product Information.

g. You authorize Amazon to obtain any Required Product Information from your inventory management system or dealer management system, as applicable, in connection with the Program.

h. **Product Availability.**

i. You will update your inventory management system or dealer management system, as applicable, within 30 minutes to indicate Your Product listed on the Amazon Site has been sold or is otherwise not available for purchase on the Amazon Site to ensure your Required Product Information is accurate. Furthermore, you will ensure that none of your employees fraudulently indicate Your Product is unavailable for purchase.

ii. Upon a customer obtaining approval or conditional approval from a lender, unless such customer otherwise completes and provides all required documentation and completes payment (if applicable), you will not sell the applicable Product (as identified by the VIN in the Product listing) to anyone other than such customer for at least 27 business hours.

iii. Upon a customer completing and providing all required documentation and completing payment (if applicable), unless such customer otherwise cancels the applicable order, you will not cancel the applicable order for at least three (3) business days.

4. **Payments**

a. For the purposes of these Dealer Requirements, the Autos Program Policies, and the Agreement the definition of “Sales Proceeds” is hereby deleted from the Agreement and replaced with the following:

“**Sales Proceeds**” means the total deposit, cash payment or down payment a customer elects to pay for a vehicle, as applicable, on the Amazon Site.”

b. Notwithstanding Section S-1.2 of the Agreement, Amazon will receive Sales Proceeds for a vehicle on your behalf for each vehicle transaction and will remit them to you in accordance with the Agreement.

5. **Shipping Exclusion**. Section S-1.3 of the Agreement will not apply.

****6.** Insurance. **Regardless of the amount of your gross proceeds, you will maintain and, if applicable, cause your affiliates (including through a third party) to maintain at all times while you participate in the Program a commercial general, umbrella or excess liability insurance with a limit of USD 1,000,000 per occurrence and in aggregate to cover liabilities caused by or occurring in conjunction with the operation of your business, including products, products/completed operations and bodily injury, with policy(ies) naming Amazon and its assignees as additional insureds. You will document the existence of proper coverage in your Seller Central Account, and keep such documentation up-to-date. You will provide Amazon with at least 30 days’ advance written notice of cancellation, significant modification, or lapse of coverage. At our request, you will provide to us certificates of insurance for the coverage by email at [coi@amazon.com](mailto:coi@amazon.com) or c/o Amazon, P.O. Box 81226, Seattle, WA 98108-1226, Attention: Risk Management.

7. **Tax Matters**. Notwithstanding Section 10 of the Agreement, you acknowledge and agree that Amazon is not obligated to determine your tax obligation, whether taxes apply, and you will be responsible for the calculation, collection, reporting, remittance and payment of any and all of Your Taxes. For the avoidance of doubt, Your Taxes include any tag, title, registration or other government fees that must be paid in connection with the sale of a vehicle.

8. **Orders**

a. Except as otherwise provided in these Dealer Requirements or the Autos Program Policies, you may not revise the order submitted by the customer on the Amazon Site except to (i) correct any errors in the taxes and fees; and/or (ii) correct any inapplicable incentives or rebates. If there any changes to the order submitted by the customer, you must inform (A) the Amazon customer support team of any such changes and (B) the customer prior to such customer signing any retail installment sales contract.

b. You will not add additional Products (e.g. protection products, financing and insurance products) to the customer’s order.

c. You instruct and authorize Amazon and its service providers to forward credit applications and credit reports to the applicable lender (which may be via your service provider), and to display any credit decision to each customer. You will be responsible for providing to each customer a credit score disclosure/risk-based pricing notice or a notice that a credit score is not available, and any notice of credit denial, as applicable.

d. You acknowledge and agree that Amazon will surface the [E-Sign Disclosure and Consent for Electronic Communications Agreement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G99H4M4FSNBVPCMW) and that you will comply with its terms.

e. You will prepare all relevant documentation for execution by customer within one (1) hour of customer’s completed submission of their order on Amazon such submission occurs during your business hours, otherwise you will prepare all relevant documentation within one (1) hour of opening the following business day.

f. You will: (i) comply with any applicable lender agreement relating to a retail installment sales contract or leasing contract that you assign to such lender for a vehicle sold through the Amazon Site; and (ii) deliver the vehicle to the customer before assigning such retail installment contract or leasing contract to a lender.

g. You will enable customers to cancel their applicable order at any point prior to pick-up or delivery of the vehicle. Upon cancellation, you will issue a full refund of any Sales Proceeds paid by such customer.

9. **Customer Service **

a. You will monitor and respond to any inquiries that come from customers through Buyer-Seller messages in Seller Central within 60 minutes during your business hours and respond within 60 minutes by the next business day for messages that are sent by customers outside of your business hours. b. You will comply with the Seller Code of Conduct under this Agreement for both Amazon online customers as well as Amazon customers that cannot complete the transaction online and contact you and otherwise engage with you directly not through the Amazon Site. c. You will respond to any Amazon requested actions related to customer issues arising during shopping journey via email within 8 business hours. d. You will maintain accurate assigned points of contact within your physical store for Amazon customers to contact and provide applicable contact information. You will keep the assigned points of contact information up to date and accurate and will provide backups in the event there is a change.

10. **Seller Code of Conduct**

**a. Communications**

i. You may not send unsolicited or inappropriate messages. Except as otherwise provided herein, unless a customer contacts you directly, prior to delivery of the vehicle, all communications to customers must be sent through Buyer-Seller Messaging and be necessary for fulfilling the order or providing customer services. You may not directly contact any customer or potential customer prior to delivery of the vehicle; provided that, you may contact any customer that submits a credit application directly via mail or e-mail solely for purpose of providing such customer with a credit score disclosure as required by applicable Law. Marketing communications are prohibited.

ii. Upon delivery of the vehicle and completion of the sale, you may communicate with any applicable customer that has purchased a vehicle from you using customer’s preferred method of communication and in accordance with your privacy policy. ** b. Customer Information.**

i. If prior to delivery of the vehicle, you receive customer information such as addresses or phone numbers, you may use that information only to fulfill orders and may not use it for marketing purposes. You will exclude any such customer information from any marketing in your CRM. ii. Notwithstanding your privacy notice and privacy policy, you will not disclose, sell, rent, trade or otherwise share any customer information with any unaffiliated third-parties (excluding any service providers or third-party lenders) except as required by federal, state or local laws.

**11. ****A to Z Guarantee.** Notwithstanding Section S-3.3 of the Agreement, the A to Z Guarantee will not apply to this Program.

**12. ****Identification Verification**. You will be responsible to verify the identity of and perform all relevant fraud checks on any customer that purchases Your Products. You are responsible if you provide possession of Your Products to a person who employs any fraudulent scheme, trick or device.

**13. Rebate Eligibility. **You will be responsible to verify eligibility for any incentives or rebates which are otherwise not available without meeting certain eligibility requirements.

**14. Applicable Law. **You will comply with all applicable Law including federal, state and local laws when listing and selling a vehicle on Amazon, all laws related to vehicle transactions and any extension of credit related thereto.

**15. Data. **Except as other directed by you or upon obtaining customer consent, Amazon will not (1) directly collect or retain any customer personal data from you, your CRM or your DMS; and (2) directly collect any deal related data from your DMS.

16. **Feedback.** You may provide feedback on the Program via surveys, meetings or any other format **(“Feedback”)**. The Feedback may cover topics related to the customer and seller experience, including the performance of, features of and learnings from the Program. Amazon will own and may use and evaluate all Feedback for its own purposes. You will not use any Feedback except for your internal evaluation purposes of the Program.

17. Used and OEM CPO.

a. Curated Selection.

i. You may list (a) Any OEM CPO vehicle and (b) Used vehicles which: (1) are 10 model years or less, with less than 100,000 miles at the time of listing on the US Amazon Site; (2) have a Limited Warranty that meets the minimum requirements in Section 17.b below; (3) are physically on your lot and not shared with another rooftop and (4) are Class 2 passenger cars with body type as sedan, SUV, hatchback, coupe, wagon, convertible or minivan or Class 3 two-axle, four-tire single unit vehicles with body type of pickup truck. “Class” means the U.S. Department of Transportation vehicle types listed here: [https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/tmguide/tmg_2013/vehicle-types.cfm](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/tmguide/tmg_2013/vehicle-types.cfm)

ii. You may only list Used or OEM CPO vehicles that you know or should reasonably know to meet the following conditions:

1. Vehicle is not marked as “Stolen”.

2. Vehicle is free of odometer rollback discrepancies.

3. Vehicle has not been marked as salvaged or rebuilt.

4. Vehicle has not been marked as damaged in flood, fire, or hail.

5. Vehicle has no history of Lemon Law Buybacks.

iii. You may not list any: (1) vehicles from a Defunct Brand (2) Ultra Luxury vehicles (3) Class 1 two or three-wheeled motorized vehicles (4) Class 3 two-axle, four-tire single unit vehicles with body type as van, panels, campers, motor homes, ambulance, hearse, carryall, minibus or RV; or (5) any Class 4 through 13 vehicles. A “**Defunct Brand”** means a vehicle brand that is no longer manufactured. **“Ultra Luxury”** means the following brands: Bentley, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin, Ferrari, Bugatti, Rolls-Royce, Maserati, Maybach, or Lotus.

b. Minimum Limited Warranty. All Used vehicles must meet or exceed each of the following Limited Warranty requirements:

1. At least 30-days / 1,000 miles (ICE, EV, + PHEV)

2. At least Powertrain mechanical breakdown/failure (Parts & Labor), includes Seals and Gaskets

3. OEM Parts or Like-Kind-Quality (LKQ)

4. Roadside Assistance and towing for a Covered Claim

5. Disappearing ($0) deductible if repaired by selling dealership (otherwise, maximum of $250 deductible)

6. Claims must be permitted at any licensed service facility

c. Buyer’s Guide. All Used and OEM CPO vehicles must have a digital copy (e.g. PDF) of the Buyer’s Guide available to provide to customers on Amazon Autos. If you do not have a digital Buyer’s Guide for each Used and OEM CPO listing, you will provide Amazon with accurate Buyer’s Guide information and hereby authorize Amazon to generate a Buyer’s Guide on your behalf using such information provided by you. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of any Buyer’s Guide, including any Buyer’s Guide you give permission to Amazon to be generated by Amazon on your behalf.

d. Service and Protection Products. You may only offer the service and protection products supported on Amazon Autos. “Service and Protection Products” means optional service contracts offered by you, such as Tire and Wheel, Extended Warranty, GAP Insurance, Pre-Paid Maintenance, that a customer may add to their vehicle purchase.

18. **Trade-Ins**

a. You will perform the confirmatory inspection using a professional On-Board Diagnostics 2 (“OBD2”) device. b. If the confirmatory inspection results in an update to the trade-in valuation a customer received on Amazon, you will inform Amazon of any such update in value. c. You will honor the trade-in valuation customer receives on the Amazon Site; provided that, if upon performing a confirmatory inspection on the trade-in vehicle, the trade-in valuation decreases by more than $750, you may update the order submitted by customer on the Amazon Site to reflect the updated valuation. You will not offer less than the updated trade-in valuation provided by ACV Auctions.

19. **Returns. **Notwithstanding Section S-2.2 of the Agreement, you will not be obligated to comply with the Amazon 30-day Return Policy. For new vehicles, you will enroll in and honor any applicable OEM return or exchange policy. For Used and OEM CPO vehicles, you will honor the [Amazon Autos Return Policy,](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G3SLKVSKAAU7TSBV) which may be updated from time to time in accordance with the Autos Program Policies.
