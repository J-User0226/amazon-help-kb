# Additional features in Seller Central

Amazon's Seller Central offers many additional features and tools to help enhance your selling experience. The videos below will demonstrate key features such as promotions, gift options, and providing access to key areas for other members of your business.

## Notification preferences
You can change the email addresses to which you want to receive notifications about your account. This video will show you how to update your **Notification Preferences**.

## Gift options
You can enable gift options for your whole catalog or only for specific SKUs. Our gift options include **Gift Messaging** and **Gift Wrap**.

## User permissions
You can edit **User Permissions** to grant multiple people access to your Seller Central account.

## Your Info & Policies
The **Your Info & Policies** section allows you to add or edit custom content about your Amazon business and policies.

## Business reports
The **Business Reports** feature lets you monitor your overall sales, product performance, traffic, and conversion rates.

## Create a Promotion
Sellers use promotions for a variety of reasons:
- Increase general sales
- Push old inventory or make room for new inventory
- Take advantage of seasonal or holiday sales
- Pass on cost savings to customers
