## Export and Import clearance
- The Amazon SEND carrier will clear customs in both India and the US on your behalf, based on the information you provided in the shipping documents. If customs duty is charged on the shipment, the Amazon SEND carrier will pay the customs duty, and the same will be deducted from your Seller Central disbursement account to be paid to the Amazon SEND carrier. The Amazon SEND carrier can contact you for any additional documents or information.
- Since your destination country is the US, make sure to follow the shipping and routing requirements listed on the [Delivering imports to Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200280280) page for delivering imports to an Amazon fulfillment center.

**Note 1: Export Declaration (India Customs)**
- If the shipment value is below INR 50,000 (general goods) and qualifies under courier mode, a formal Shipping Bill may not be required. However, a commercial invoice with HSN code (Customs Tariff Number) is mandatory.
- If the shipment value is above INR 50,000 (or as per applicable export regulations), a Shipping Bill (Export Declaration) must be filed with Indian Customs through ICEGATE before Xhipment can clear the shipment.
- Certain categories, such as restricted goods, high-value shipments, or specific product types may require mandatory export documentation regardless of value.

Before requesting the Export Declaration, ensure the following
- Complete the shipment creation workflow in Seller Central.
- The destination address, that is Amazon fulfillment center is confirmed, as this is mandatory for filing the Shipping Bill.

Additional information required for Export Declaration:
- **IEC (Import Export Code)** of the exporter
- **GST Number**
- **AD Code registration** at the port of export
- **HSN Code**
- Commercial Invoice & Packing List
- Authorized Dealer Bank details
- Port of Loading (depends on Xhipment gateway location)

Shipping Bill is filed electronically through ICEGATE (Indian Customs EDI System).

These are the two options to file Export Declaration
1. **Self-Directed Filing**
  - File the Shipping Bill through your appointed Customs Broker (CHA).
  - Provide the Let Export Order (LEO) copy to Xhipment at pickup.

2. **Via Service Provider**
  - You may appoint a third-party Customs Broker or service provider who can complete the Shipping Bill filing on your behalf (typically within 2 to 3 working days, subject to document readiness).

**Note*2 Import of Record (IOR) Xhipment**

**Xhipment**
Situations if Amazon SEND Carrier will act as IOR
- Xhipment **will** act as the IOR for shipments with value less than $50,000 and the following conditions are met:
  1. The commodities are **not** regulated by a US Government agency, such as FDA, EPA, FWS, DOT.
  2. The commodities are not subject to anti-dumping or countervailing duties.

- Xhipment **will not** act as the IOR for shipments with value more than $500,000 or less than $500,000 but are not meeting the aforementioned criteria (1) and (2).

Process of getting IOR from Amazon SEND carrier
Xhipment can assist the seller with establishing a non-resident importer (NRI) account and customs bond, which will be required before the release of the good. Follow these steps for creating a shipment in this scenario:
1. Seller to register a Xhipment account on Xhipment website.
2. Fill out a continuous bond application and Power of Attorney form.
3. Once the documents have been processed you will receive a non-resident importer number from Xhipment. Add this number to the commercial invoice.

## Lost shipment claims
Your shipments may get lost due to uncontrollable reasons, and you can raise a claim with the carrier directly to initiate the investigation. To raise an investigation, contact the seller through the customer support information mention in the **Amazon SEND carrier shipping requirements** section on this page.

To file a claim, go to [UPS Claim](https://www.ups.com/us/en/support/file-a-claim).

**Note: **Amazon does not participate in claims between UPS and sellers.

## Shipments with additional clearance requirements
Your shipments may require additional import clearances. We have outlined some of the most common requirements you may encounter:

**FDA Requirements**: You must get FDA registration for products related to Animal and Veterinary, Drugs, Food, Medical Devices, Radiation-Emitting Products. For information about the registration process related to your commodity, go to [US FDA website](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-basics-industry/registration-and-listing).

**Prior Notice**: FDA must be notified in advance of any shipments of food for humans and other animals that are imported into the US, unless the food is exempt from Prior Notice. For the purpose of prior notice “food” is defined as Articles used for food or drink for man or other animals, chewing gum or Articles used for components of these items. For information about the prior notice process, go to [FDA Prior System](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-import-process/prior-notice-imported-foods). All information must be included on the commercial invoice.

**TSCA**: Imports of chemical substances, ink in pens, composite wood products, mixtures or articles that contain a chemical substance, or mixture must comply with the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA) in order to enter the US. The TSCA certificate must be provided by an authorized entity with an address in USA. For more information, go to [TSCA Requirements for Importing Chemicals](https://www.epa.gov/tsca-import-export-requirements/tsca-requirements-importing-chemicals#positive).

**MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet)**: If your shipment contains chemicals or batteries, you must provide MSDS certificates for each product. Generally, the MSDS of chemicals are included in the packaging. If not, you can ask the supplier or manufacturer for the same.

**Radiation declaration**: If you send electronic products which fall under Radiation Control Standards, the form FDA 2877 must be completed and shared with customs along with the export documents. For information about the complete list, go to [A-Z List of Radiation Regulated Products and Procedures](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/radiation-emitting-products-and-procedures/z-list-regulated-products-procedures).
