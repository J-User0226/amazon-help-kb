# Error 18729

## Error message

${variant} image contains prohibited badging. To fix this: (1) remove all badges, awards, or certification marks from image; (2) ensure image meets Product Image Requirements; (3) resubmit compliant image. For more details, search for error code or error message in the help section of your account.

## Reason for error 18729

This error occurs for the following reasons:

- The product image includes badges, awards, or certification marks that are not permitted in Amazon listings.

- Images feature unauthorized use of trademarked badges or symbols from organizations or events.

- The product photo contains watermarks, rating symbols, or customer review badges.

- The image does not comply with Amazon's Product Image Requirements regarding the use of additional graphic elements.

## Resolve error 18729

To resolve the error, follow these steps:

- Review your image carefully against the detailed [Amazon's Product Image Requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

- Remove all badges, awards, and certification marks from the product image.

- Ensure that your image focuses solely on presenting the product without any additional graphic overlays or seals.

- Resubmit the compliant image that accurately represents your product without any restricted content.
