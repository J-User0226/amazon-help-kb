## Export and Import clearance

- The partnered carrier will clear customs in both UK and the US on your behalf, based on the information you provided in the shipping documents. If customs duty is charged on the shipment, the partnered carrier will pay the customs duty, and the same will be deducted from your Seller Central disbursement account to be paid to the Amazon SEND carrier. The Amazon SEND carrier can contact you for any additional documents or information.

- Since your destination country is the US, make sure to follow the shipping and routing requirements listed on the [Delivering imports to Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200280280) page for delivering imports to an Amazon fulfillment center.

## Shipments with additional clearance requirements

Your shipments may require additional import clearances. We have outlined some of the most common requirements you may encounter:

**FDA Requirements**: You must get FDA registration for products related to Animal and Veterinary, Drugs, Food, Medical Devices, Radiation-Emitting Products. For information about the registration process related to your commodity, go to [US FDA website](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-basics-industry/registration-and-listing).

**Prior Notice**: FDA must be notified in advance of any shipments of food for humans and other animals that are imported into the US, unless the food is exempt from Prior Notice. For the purpose of prior notice “food” is defined as Articles used for food or drink for man or other animals, chewing gum or Articles used for components of these items. For information about the prior notice process, go to [FDA Prior System](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-import-process/prior-notice-imported-foods). All information must be included on the commercial invoice.

**TSCA**: Imports of chemical substances, ink in pens, composite wood products, mixtures or articles that contain a chemical substance, or mixture must comply with the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA) in order to enter the US. The TSCA certificate must be provided by an authorized entity with an address in USA. For more information, go to [TSCA Requirements for Importing Chemicals](https://www.epa.gov/tsca-import-export-requirements/tsca-requirements-importing-chemicals#positive).

**MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet)**: If your shipment contains chemicals or batteries, you must provide MSDS certificates for each product. Generally, the MSDS of chemicals are included in the packaging. If not, you can ask the supplier or manufacturer for the same.

**Radiation declaration**: If you send electronic products which fall under Radiation Control Standards, the form FDA 2877 must be completed and shared with customs along with the export documents. For information about the complete list, go to [A-Z List of Radiation Regulated Products and Procedures](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/radiation-emitting-products-and-procedures/z-list-regulated-products-procedures).
