# Target Inventory for Peak Success (TIPS) Program Terms and Conditions

These Terms and Conditions (“Terms and Conditions”) apply to your participation in the Target Inventory for Peak Success (TIPS) program (“Program”). The Terms and Conditions supplement the [Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1791) (the “Agreement”).

- This program is only available to sellers who have received an invitation directly from Amazon via email.
- The TIPS Program will run during the following dates:
  - From October 1, 2026, through December 31, 2026
  - Benefits related to removal fees will run until April 30, 2027

- Benefits identified in the Program (the “Benefits”) are only available for the specified store(s) and selected product(s), as identified by Amazon in the email invitation.
- To receive a Benefit, you must complete all eligibility steps outlined in the email invitation within the time specified by Amazon.
- Upon your completion of the eligibility steps, the Benefits associated with the Program will be applied to your seller account via the method described in the invitation email and any Program materials.
- Any Benefit provided to you is non-transferable, not for resale, and not redeemable for cash.
- If you violate any of these Terms and Conditions or the Agreement, the invitation to participate will be invalid and Amazon may void and reverse any Benefit that was applied to your account as part of the Program.
- Amazon may terminate or modify these Terms and Conditions, change or cancel the existing Program or Benefits, or cancel your participation in the Program at any time.
- Any information you receive about the Program is confidential. The confidentiality provisions of the Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement apply.
