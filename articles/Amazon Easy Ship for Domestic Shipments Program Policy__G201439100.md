# Amazon Easy Ship for Domestic Shipments Program Policy

Amazon Easy Ship (**AES**) provides shipping, customer service, and associated services for Seller-Fulfilled Products within the United States (AES for Domestic Shipments). This AES for Domestic Shipments Program Policy (**AES Domestic Policy**) is part of the Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement (**Agreement**), and, unless specifically provided otherwise, concerns and applies only to your participation in AES for Domestic Shipments. BY REGISTERING FOR OR USING AES FOR DOMESTIC SHIPMENTS, YOU (ON BEHALF OF YOURSELF OR THE BUSINESS YOU REPRESENT) AGREE TO BE BOUND BY THE AGREEMENT, INCLUDING THIS AES POLICY AND APPLICABLE PROGRAM POLICIES.

Capitalized terms not defined in this AES Domestic Policy have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

You expressly agree that Amazon may engage its Affiliate(s) or a third party (each, a **Shipping Partner**) in order to complete one or more of the services outlined below.

**1 Your Products**

AES Excluded Products, as well as any product that violates applicable Program Policies, are not eligible to be shipped using AES for Domestic Shipments. You are responsible for determining whether your Seller-Fulfilled Products are eligible for domestic shipping through AES for Domestic Shipments, and you can unenroll products from AES for Domestic Shipments via Seller Central. Amazon may disallow any Unit it believes is ineligible for domestic shipment under Amazon policy.

**2 Product and Shipping Information**

You will, in accordance with Section S-1 of the Agreement and any applicable Program Policies, provide accurate and complete information, including Shipping Information, about Units that we determine is required to enable the sale or shipment of Units, and will update your Units (including packaging) as determined by us to enable their sale or shipment. You will promptly update any information about Units as needed or as requested by us so that the information is at all times accurate and complete.

**3 Shipping**

3.1 We will collect Units from your AES Address and arrange shipment to the shipping addresses in the United States included in valid customer orders. We may ship Units together with products purchased from other merchants, including any of our Affiliates. You acknowledge and agree that, as reasonably necessary in the course of providing AES services, Units may be opened and inspected.

3.2 You are the shipper of record for Units shipped through AES for Domestic Shipments and are responsible for the contents of each package. You will only ship Units that are fit for carriage, comply with all applicable marking, labeling and other requirements required by Law, comply with our requirements and policies, and are properly packaged for the purposes of carriage, including for protection against damage and deterioration during shipment. You will not ship any Unsuitable Unit or items prohibited under our policies, and we may reject any Unit on reasonable grounds including: (a) that the shipment contains Unsuitable Units or does not comply with the Agreement and any applicable policies; (b) where you are not identified as the shipper in the transport documentation; or (c) to enable us, and our Shipping Partners, to manage business operations. You expressly agree that for all purposes, including as required by Law, that you are the shipper of the Units and take all steps that we may reasonably require to give effect to that relationship.

**4 Customer Service and Returns**

4.1 Amazon will provide customer service for Units on your behalf through Customer Service by Amazon. By participating in AES for Domestic Shipments, you agree to the Customer Service by Amazon Terms and Conditions. You may not withdraw from CSBA while participating in AES for Domestic Shipments, but may unenroll products from AES for Domestic Shipments as described in Section 1.

4.2 In the event of customer returns or where an item cannot be delivered, if the Unit is returnable and you have a valid return address in the United States, Amazon will facilitate the return of the Unit back to you. If the Unit is returnable and you do not have a valid return address in the United States, Amazon will generate a return label for the customer to return the Unit to you. You will have two (2) business days upon receiving the returned Unit to action a customer refund. If you fail to provide the shipping label or action the refund within this period, you agree that, to preserve a great customer experience, Amazon will automatically issue a refund to the relevant customer and deduct those funds from your seller account. If the Unit is not returnable (e.g., hazardous in nature), you agree that Amazon may automatically refund the customer and deduct those funds from your seller account. You also agree that Units shipped through Domestic AES are eligible for returnless refund resolutions ("Returnless Resolution") in accordance with Amazon’s returnless refunds policy, and you agree that Amazon may provide customers a Returnless Resolution instead of requiring Units to be returned.

4.3 This Section 4 does not limit any rights a customer may have, and any additional obligations you have including at law or under any express warranty you offer. We will promptly notify you when you are responsible for a customer refund. You will have the opportunity to appeal any refund issued by Amazon under this AES Policy via the SAFE-T tool or any other means specified by Amazon. Amazon will reasonably consider any appeal and documentation that you submit.

**5 Returns to You and Disposal**

5.1 If: (a) the AES Return Address we have for you is outdated or incorrect; or (b) you have not, upon our request, confirmed a valid AES Return Address, then the Unit(s) will be deemed abandoned and we may elect to dispose of them. We require these rights to enable us, and our Shipping Partners, to manage business operations.

5.2 We may dispose of any Unit (and you will be deemed to have consented to our action) immediately if we determine that the Unit creates a safety, health, or liability risk to Amazon, our personnel, or any third party. We also may dispose of any Unit that is an AES Excluded Product or for which you fail to promptly provide information as required under this AES Domestic Policy. You agree to reimburse us for the reasonable expenses we incur in connection with the return or disposal of any Units under this Section 5.

5.3 Where we have the right to dispose of Units, we may dispose of such Units as appropriate based on the inventory (for example, by selling, recycling, donating, or destroying it) and retain any proceeds we receive from the disposal. Title to each disposed Unit will transfer to us (or a third party we select such as a charity) at no cost to us as necessary for us to dispose of the Unit, however this will not affect any liability you have or any indemnity you provide to us in respect of such Unit. You will have no security interest, lien, or other claim in connection with the disposal of these Units.

**6 Reimbursement for Lost/Damaged Units**

In situations where, after the Units are collected, the wrong Unit is delivered to the customer or the Unit is damaged or lost or is missing, unless caused by you or any of your employees, agents, or contractors, we will reimburse you in accordance with the "Amazon Easy Ship Lost and Damaged Inventory Reimbursement Policy" for the relevant Unit (and you will, at our request, provide us a valid tax invoice for the amount reimbursed to you where applicable).

You will pay us the applicable fees described in the [Amazon Easy Ship Fees Schedule](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G201379720). You will be charged the fees with respect to each Unit once the Unit is handed over to us or our Shipping Partner for delivery. **AES Definitions**

"**AES Domestic Address**" means the designated address within the United States that you provided to Amazon for the collection of your Units via Seller Central.

"**AES Return Address**" means the designated United States address that you provided to Amazon for the return of your Unit via Seller Central.

"**AES Excluded Product**" means any Unit that is an Excluded Product or is otherwise prohibited by applicable Program Policies or [Amazon Easy Ship Restrictions.](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G201381980)

"**Seller Agreement**" means the Selling on Amazon Service Terms including applicable policies.

"**Shipping Information**" means with respect to any purchased Unit(s), the following information: the name of the recipient, the shipping address, the quantity of Units to be shipped, the weight and dimension of the relevant package in which the Unit is shipped, and any other shipping-related information we may reasonably request.

"**Unit**" means a unit of Your Product that you ship in connection with AES for Domestic Deliveries.

"**Unsuitable Unit**" means a Unit that Amazon reasonably determines: (a) is defective, damaged, unfit for a particular purpose, or lacking required label(s); (b) bears labels that were not properly registered with Amazon before shipment or do not match the product that was registered; (c) is an AES Excluded Product; (d) does not comply with Law or the Agreement; or (e) is unsellable or undeliverable, for example having regard to requirements associated with handling or shipping of the Unit.
