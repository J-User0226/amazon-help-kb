# Amazon autos return policy

By participating in the Amazon Autos program, you agree to adhere to this return policy. Compliance with this policy is essential to maintain the integrity of the Amazon Autos platform and ensure customer satisfaction.

1. **Return Eligibility and Window**.
  1. This return policy applies exclusively to Used and CPO vehicles sold through Amazon.
  2. You must accept returns for all Used and CPO vehicles sold through Amazon Autos if the following conditions are met:
    1. The return request is initiated within 3 days from the time the vehicle is marked delivered on A2Z. While the request must be made within this 3-day window, the actual return may occur beyond this period.
    2. The difference between odometer readings at pickup and the return drop-off does not exceed 300 miles.
    3. The vehicle is in essentially the same condition as it was at the time of sale. This means:
      1. The vehicle has not been involved in any accident or sustained any damage since the time of purchase.
      2. The vehicle has not been altered from the condition it was sold in.
      3. No new liens or legal claims have been placed on the vehicle, except for any financing lien related to the customer's purchase of this specific vehicle

    4. You cannot refuse a return if all the above conditions are met.

  3. The return window begins when you mark the vehicle as delivered on A2Z after the customer has completed paperwork and driven off the lot. The return window ends at 11:59 PM (local time) on the third calendar day following the delivery date.
  4. The three-day return window is based on calendar days, not business days. This includes weekends and holidays. Example: If a vehicle is marked as delivered on A2Z at 2:00 PM on Friday, the return window ends at 11:59 PM on Monday, regardless of whether Monday is a holiday or if the dealership is closed on Sunday.
  5. The delivery date (Day 0) is not counted as one of the three days in the return window.

2. **Return Process**.
  1. Amazon will automatically notify you of return requests via email and Seller Central alerts.
  2. The return request will include:
    1. The return reason.
    2. Additional notes from the Customer Service Associate (CSA) who submitted the return request on behalf of the customer.
    3. The date and time the customer wants to drop off the vehicle.

  3. The drop-off appointment will be scheduled within 48 hours of receiving the return request. You must honor the scheduled drop-off appointment or arrange a drop-off appointment rescheduling by contacting Amazon Autos' Customer Support or contacting the customer directly via Buyer-Seller Messages on Seller Central.
  4. Drop-off Appointment Rescheduling:
    1. Rescheduling can only be arranged at least 4 operating hours before the initially scheduled appointment time.
    2. Any rescheduled appointment must still occur within 48 hours of the original return request. If it's less than 4 operating hours before the scheduled appointment, you must honor the original scheduled time.

  5. You can inspect the vehicle during the return appointment to validate its return eligibility.

3. **Return Rejection**.
  1. If one or more of the conditions in Section 1b appear not to be met, and you are considering rejecting the return request, you must:
    1. Report in Seller Central immediately by initiating the decline request workflow.
    2. Select the most appropriate reason for rejection from the provided options.
    3. Provide detailed additional context in the free text field explaining why the return conditions weren't met.

  2. You must retain all documentation related to the return rejection, including photographs and detailed notes, for potential review by Amazon.
  3. Amazon may request proof of these discrepancies in case of customer escalation and if an investigation is required.
  4. Note that Amazon may review rejected returns and overturn decisions if the evidence is insufficient or the rejection is deemed unjustified.

4. **Refund Processing**. Upon accepting a return, you must:
  1. Issue a refund of any principal amount paid at the dealership during pickup, including any positive equity from a trade-in. The principal amount is equal to the sum the customer paid directly to you during vehicle pickup, as well as any positive equity from a trade-in vehicle.
    1. If the customer paid in full, you will refund them via check.
    2. If the customer financed the vehicle, you will work with them to unwind the financing contract and refund any amounts owed.

  2. Process the refund of the down payment made on Amazon through Seller Central.
  3. All refunds must be processed within 24 hours of accepting the return.
  4. You are responsible for unwinding any financing arrangements before the first month payment if a return is processed.
  5. You are responsible for unwinding any additional protection products contracted during the sale, such as extended warranties, gap insurance, or service contracts.

5. **Trade-Ins**.
  1. While holding trade-ins during the return window is strongly recommended, you may handle trade-ins according to their own policies and in compliance with applicable law and the Autos Program Policies.
  2. For trade-in policy inquiries related to returns, customers will be directed to contact you directly via phone or through Amazon's BSM channel.
  3. You are required to monitor any inquiries that come from customers through BSM in Seller Central within 24 hours on weekdays and by the next business day on weekends and holidays.
  4. In the event of a return with a trade-in, you must either refund the full trade-in equity or return the trade-in vehicle to the customer.

6. **Exceptions and Escalations**.
  1. In cases where a return is initiated after 3 days but within 400 miles and 5 days, you may be asked to process a return exception if specific criteria are met.
  2. Examples of circumstances that may justify a return exception include, but are not limited to:
    1. Verifiable technical issues with Amazon's systems that prevented the customer from initiating a return within the 3-day window.
    2. Documented severe weather conditions or natural disasters that prevented the customer from returning the vehicle within the specified timeframe.
    3. Unexpected hospitalization of the customer or immediate family member, supported by medical documentation.
    4. Discovery of a significant mechanical issue that was not disclosed in the vehicle's inspection report and could not have been reasonably detected within the first 3 days of ownership.
    5. Verifiable errors in the vehicle's documentation or title that were not apparent at the time of purchase and discovered after the 3-day window.
    6. Instances where you failed to provide necessary information or documentation required for the customer to make an informed decision within the 3-day window.
      1. All exception requests must be thoroughly documented and will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by Amazon Autos.
      2. The final decision on whether to grant an exception rests with Amazon Autos, and you are expected to comply with this decision.
      3. You must cooperate with Amazon's investigation process for any escalated return cases, including providing all requested documentation and evidence related to vehicle condition discrepancies or exception criteria.

7. **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)**.
  1. Standard Returns (5-Day SLA):
    1. Customers must initiate their return request within 3 days of vehicle pick-up.
    2. Once initiated, customers must physically return the vehicle within 48 hours.
    3. Customers may reschedule once if they contact AACS prior to their original return appointment. The new appointment must be within 48 hours of the original.
    4. You must process the refund flow immediately upon receiving the returned vehicle to ensure prompt customer reimbursement.

  2. Disputed Returns (48-Hour SLA):
    1. In cases of disputed returns, Amazon will conduct an expedited investigation and resolution process within 48 hours to minimize customer uncertainty.

  3. Your Responsibilities:
    1. You must complete the return process, including refund processing, within 24 hours of accepting the return.
    2. In cases where you close a return without issuing a refund, you must provide a detailed explanation within 1 hour.
    3. If a refund is not issued within 30 minutes of the return appointment, you must provide an explanation and expected resolution time.

  4. Monitoring and Escalation:
    1. Amazon will create hourly tracking mechanisms for open return requests to ensure all SLAs are being adhered to.
    2. Real-time alarms will notify Amazon of potential critical failures, such as:
      1. When you close a return without issuing a refund.
      2. When a refund is not issued within 30 minutes of the return appointment.
      3. These alarms will trigger AACS to contact the customer and/or you to resolve the issue.
      4. Persistent SLA violations may result in escalation to the Account Manager and Compliance teams.

8. **Compliance and Penalties**.
  1. Failure to comply with this return policy, including refusing eligible returns, may result in penalties, including but not limited to (i) Temporary suspension from the Amazon Autos program and/or (ii) Termination of the [Amazon Autos Seller agreement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/GXE3YGZ54YHJ6RB9).
  2. Amazon reserves the right to resolve any complaints that you have improperly refused as it reasonably deems necessary and in Amazon's sole discretion. In the event that Amazon incurs additional costs or expenses in connection with such a resolution, Amazon may collect those costs from you.

9. **Record keeping**.
  1. You must maintain accurate records of all returns, including:
    1. Inspection reports.
    2. Communication logs with customers.
    3. Refund documentation.

10. **Policy Updates**.
  1. You will be notified of policy updates in accordance with Section 8 (Modification) of the Autos Program Policies.
