# Error 100587

## Error message

The ${variant} image you submitted for ${ou_name} has a human model for a non-clothing product which is not permitted for this product type in ${marketplace} store. As a result, your image(s) will not be considered for display to customers, but this will not lead to ASIN suppression. No action is required at this time, but if you choose to upload a compliant image, please note that its display is not guaranteed. For more information on image guidelines, please visit the Image Issues page.

## Reason for error 100587

This error occurs for the following reasons:

- Product images feature human models displaying or demonstrating non-clothing items, which violate marketplace image guidelines.

- Product photography includes hands, faces, or other human elements when showcasing non-apparel items.

- Images contain human models for categories like electronics, home goods, toys, or other non-clothing merchandise.

## Resolve error 100587

To resolve the error, follow these steps:

- Retake product photos showing only the item itself without any packaging, boxes, or retail containers. Ensure the product is displayed on a pure white background and follows [Amazon's product image requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

- Create standalone product images that focus solely on the product's features, design, and details without human demonstration or interaction.

- Resubmit the compliant image that accurately represents your product.
