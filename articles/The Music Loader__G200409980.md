# The Music Loader

To help you sell music on Amazon, we offer a specific tool to upload multiple listings at one
time: the Music Loader. The Music Loader will either match your listing data to an existing
product page in the Amazon catalogue or create a new product detail page if none exists.

## **Music Loader Features**

If a detail page for your product already exists in the Amazon.fr catalog, the Music Loader
will use the product identifier (UPC or EAN) you provide to find the match and create your
listing. Learn more about [Product
Matching](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1471).

If the Music Loader cannot find a match in the catalogue, and you provide enough data in
the upload file, Amazon will create a new product page automatically. Once a page is
created, your inventory is "matched" to the page to create an offering for buyers.

If the system does not have enough information to match to an existing page or create a new
page, you will receive an error message in the processing report following your upload.

The Music Loader template contains macros that will greatly simplify and enhance your
experience when you upload your product information. For more about these macros, see [Music Loader
Instructions](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200409990).
