# E-Sign disclosure and consent for electronic communications agreement

**Introduction**

In order to conduct business using electronic communications, to receive any document or communication electronically, and to use your electronic signature in lieu of signing paper documents, the selling dealer (“Seller”) is providing this written notice and request for consent. (All communications, notices and documents subject to this Agreement shall be defined collectively as “Communications”).

**Consent**

You confirm that you have read this Agreement and understand you are consenting to transact business electronically in connection with your motor vehicle purchase transaction, including your credit application submission (the “Transaction”). Accordingly, in regard to all Communications related to this Transaction, you consent to using electronic communications, to receive notices and disclosures electronically instead of in paper, and to use electronic signatures. You further acknowledge and agree your electronic signature is legally binding.

**Withdrawal of Consent; Consequences of withdrawing consent; How to give notice of withdrawal.**

You have the right to withdraw your consent to receive electronic Communications at any time. There are no consequences (including no fees) for withdrawing consent beyond a likely delay in receiving Communications. If you wish to do so, please contact the Seller via the process outlined [here](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLC8ZMBWMBTR6QZZ) or directly and provide your name, mailing address, daytime telephone number, and a description of the document or communication from which you are withdrawing your consent. If you are withdrawing consent to receive all electronic Communications moving forward you must notify the Seller of such intent in your writing.

**Right to Receive Paper Copies**

You have the right to receive Communications previously provided to you in electronic form in paper form. To request a paper copy of any Communication at no charge, please contact the Seller via the process outlined [here](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLC8ZMBWMBTR6QZZ) or directly, specifying in detail the Communication you would like to receive. For the avoidance of doubt, requesting a paper copy of any Communication, in and of itself, will not be treated as withdrawal of consent to receive electronic Communications.

**Required Hardware and Software**

The following hardware and software is required in order to conduct electronic Transactions:
• Internet access with an up-to-date standards-compliant web-browser that supports the HTTPS protocol, HTML, and cookies
• A PDF reader such as Adobe Reader or similar
• A connected printer to print documents and/or or sufficient computer disk space to save copies of documents

**Changes to Required Hardware or Software**

You will be notified of any changes to the hardware and/or software requirements that may create a risk that you will not be able to receive, view, print, or save a Communication.

**Changing Your Contact Information**

Should you desire to change the email address or U.S. Postal Address where the Seller should send notices and disclosures to you, you must contact the Seller via the process outlined [here](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLC8ZMBWMBTR6QZZ) or directly to make such request. Please include your previous e-mail address or U.S. Postal Address, and your updated email address or U.S. Postal Address.
