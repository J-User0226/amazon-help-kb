# Error 100584

## Error message

The ${variant} image you submitted for ${ou_name} has the product in packaging or brand/swing tags which is not permitted for this product type in ${marketplace} store. As a result, your image(s) will not be considered for display to customers, but this will not lead to ASIN suppression. No action is required at this time, but if you choose to upload a compliant image, please note that its display is not guaranteed. For more information on image guidelines, please visit the Image Issues page.

## Reason for error 100584

This error occurs for the following reasons:

- The submitted product image shows items in their retail packaging, boxes, or containers instead of the product alone.

- Product images include visible brand tags, price tags, or swing tags attached to the item.

- The images show promotional stickers, security tags, or manufacturer labels that should not be visible in the main product image.

## Resolve error 100584

To resolve the error, follow these steps:

- Retake product photos showing only the item itself without any packaging, boxes, or retail containers. Ensure the product is displayed on a pure white background and follows [Amazon's product image requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

- Remove all hanging tags, price stickers, and brand labels before photographing the product. This includes any swing tags on clothing or accessories.

- Resubmit the compliant image that accurately represents your product.
