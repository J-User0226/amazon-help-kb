# Error 18685

## Error message

Trademark Text Misuse ${brand}

## Reason for error 18685

This error occurs for the following reasons:

- You use a trademarked brand name or logo without proper authorization or rights to sell the product.

- You used trademarked symbols (™, ®) or brand names without authorization. For example, Apple™ iPhone charger.

- Product descriptions or titles include brand names that you aren’t authorized to represent.

## Resolve error 18685

To resolve the error, follow these steps:

- Go to the [Account Health Dashboard](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/performance/dashboard) to view detailed information about the trademark violation.

- Review all product listings and remove any unauthorized trademark or brand names.

- Delete or update any product titles, descriptions, and bullet points containing unauthorized trademark references.

- Obtain proper documentation proving your authorization to sell the branded products such as invoices from authorized distributors, brand authorization letters, or direct brand partnership agreements.

- For more information, go to [Product title requirements and guidelines](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC) and [Product bullet points requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/GX5L8BF8GLMML6CX).
