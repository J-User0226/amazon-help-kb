# Bank account information

Amazon uses electronic transfers to pay your sales proceeds to you. Before you can receive payments, your seller account must specify a bank account for us to deposit money into. We cannot transfer funds to a credit card or online payment system such as PayPal.

To avoid payment delays:
- Your seller account must have a [business address](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/841) associated with it.
- Your bank account must be located in a country supported by the [Amazon Currency Converter](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200497780).
- Your bank account information must be up to date.

You can use the Amazon Currency Converter service to register a bank account that is located in a different country from the marketplace where you are selling. See the [Amazon Currency Converter for Sellers FAQ](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200381250) for details.

**Note: **Amazon's Seller Assistant can provide help with this topic. For most language settings, the Seller Assistant can also directly connect you to Selling Partner Support.
